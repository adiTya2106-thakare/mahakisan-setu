The Ground Reality & Problem

Why Maharashtra Smallholders Suffer Distress Sales

  • Price Opacity: Farmers have zero visibility of processor/exporter prices in real-time.
  • Distress Selling: Immediate post-harvest cash needs force bottom-rate sales to village aggregators.
  • Quality Spec Barrier: Smallholders lack standardized grading certification, so buyers reject or discount lots.
  • Default Fear: Unverified buyers delay payments up to 45 days with check bounce risk.
MahaKisan Setu Solution
